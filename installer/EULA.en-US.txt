# 2. Administrative tool: deletions are permanent

DiskHawk is a tool for system administrators. It runs with administrator rights and can permanently delete files
and folders on the local computer and on remote computers. Deleted items do not go to the Recycle Bin and
DiskHawk cannot restore them. Review every deletion before you confirm it, try the software in a test environment
first and keep current backups.

# 3. Authorized use only

Use DiskHawk only on computers and networks that you own or are authorized to administer, and in accordance with
applicable law and your organization's policies. To scan or clean a remote computer, DiskHawk copies a small
scanner program (DiskHawk.Scanner.exe) to an administrators-only folder under C:\Windows\DiskHawk on that
computer, runs it through WMI with the credentials of the account running DiskHawk and removes it afterwards.
Endpoint protection products may flag this behavior; get approval from your security team before you use
DiskHawk on a network.

# 4. Data and privacy

DiskHawk collects no telemetry and sends no data to its authors or to any third party. Scan reports, settings,
machine lists, logs and the deletion audit log are stored on the computer where DiskHawk runs (by default in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\DiskHawk) or in folders you choose. These files can contain computer names, user names and file and
folder names, which may be personal data; protect and retain them in line with the data protection laws that
apply to you.
